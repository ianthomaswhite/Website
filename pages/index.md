## About

This site serves as a central clearinghouse for my research papers, writing samples, notes, 
and ongoing software projects. My work spans historical linguistics (specifically Proto-Indo-European 
phonology and morphology), formal systems, and building robust software with strong static guarantees.

### Current Explorations & Projects

- **Indo-European Linguistics**: Compiling an IPA-based comparative Indo-European lexicon and etymological database.
- **Functional Architecture**: Designing resilient, type-safe services and applications using Haskell.
- **AI & Reasoning**: Exploring generative tools and automated workflows for document synthesis and academic research.
